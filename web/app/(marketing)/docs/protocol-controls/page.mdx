# Protocol controls

Most of BALLAST has no admin key at all. `BallastFactory`, `BallastSeeder`,
and `BallastHook` have no owner-only function anywhere in their source — once
deployed, nobody, including us, can change how a launch, its pool, or its fee
mechanics work. `BackingLens` is the same.

Two contracts do keep an owner, because a permanently frozen version of them
would be worse than a governed one:

## FeeConfig

- **`setParams`** — the swap fee and its 3-way split. Hard-capped at 10% by
  an immutable constant; cannot go higher no matter who holds the key.
- **`setPlatformVault`** — where the platform's fee share is sent.
- **`setReferrer`** — the referrer allowlist.

## AssetRegistry

- **`setAsset` / `removeAsset`** — which assets a treasury may hold. Removing
  an asset never affects a treasury that already holds it — it only stops new
  deposits of that asset.

## Who holds these keys right now

Read live, on this page, from chain — not a static claim. Until ownership
moves to a timelock, both are held by a single wallet, and this page says so
plainly rather than implying otherwise.

## Where this is going

A 7-day `TimelockController` (standard OpenZeppelin contract, no custom
logic), proposer and executor both a multisig, admin renounced at deploy so
the timelock only ever governs itself. Once ownership moves, any change to
either contract becomes public the moment it's proposed, with 7 days before
it can execute — long enough for anyone watching to react. This page will
show the delay live once that transfer happens; until then it says "Direct
EOA — not yet timelocked," honestly.

Full technical accounting (contract addresses, verified on-chain reads, the
exact transfer steps): see the project repository's `docs/PROTOCOL_CONTROLS.md`.
