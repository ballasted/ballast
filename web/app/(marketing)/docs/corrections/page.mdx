# Corrections

BALLAST's whole pitch is accuracy, so when we get something wrong we **record it
here with a date** instead of quietly editing it away. A disclosure product should
not be able to rewrite its own history. This is the complete list, newest first.

## Upload consent said "moderated"; nothing was — visible 24–31 July 2026

From **24 July 2026** (the first version of the create flow) until **31 July
2026**, the logo-upload consent checkbox read, verbatim: _"I understand that
selected artwork will be moderated and uploaded to public IPFS."_ **Nothing ever
moderated uploads** — there was no review of any kind — so for roughly a week that
claim was live and untrue. The word was removed on 31 July 2026 when the create
flow was rebuilt, and has not been live since.

We record the **window it was visible**, not merely when a commit changed it,
because what matters to anyone reading this is how long something wrong was in
front of people — not our version-control history.

On **4 August 2026** we finished the job: the consent copy was made affirmatively
honest — _"the logo is uploaded to public IPFS, is permanent, and isn't reviewed by
BALLAST before it appears"_ — and in the same pass we stopped accepting SVG uploads
(an SVG is an active document that can carry scripts), added server-side validation
of the actual image bytes, and introduced the owner-managed
[metadata denylist](/docs/content-policy) so we can withhold impersonation branding
we would otherwise serve under our own domain.

## 28 July 2026 — Launch factory redeployed (freshness gate)

The launch factory was redeployed with a corrected freshness gate for backed
launches. **$BALLAST was not relaunched**: it remains the original launch from the
first factory and is still listed through the multi-factory read union. Only new
launches use the new factory; nothing about $BALLAST changed on-chain.

## 28 July 2026 — $BALLAST protocol-token disclosure

This site previously stated that $BALLAST was **not** a protocol token. That was
corrected — before any trading occurred — to state plainly that $BALLAST is the
protocol's own token, launched by the BALLAST team under exactly the same rules as
every other launch (100% of supply seeded the pool, no presale, no team
allocation, the team holds none of it), and that it confers no ownership, revenue
share, governance, or claim.
