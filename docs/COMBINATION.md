# How a Ballast launch works

Three independent choices, one rule that never changes.

## The three choices

**Pay with.** Buy a Ballast token with ETH by default, or any other token
that has a verified route to it. No route, no option shown — never a broken
attempt.

**Pair with.** Every launch's pool is quoted against WETH, a stock token, or
both (up to two). Two pools means the same total value split across two
books, not doubled.

**Back with.** A creator can deposit real, verifiable holdings of one
allowlisted stock token into the launch's treasury — or deposit nothing.
Holding the Ballast token gives no claim, redemption right, or entitlement
to those assets. A creator can only withdraw what they personally deposited;
anything anyone else sends stays locked forever.

## The one rule: every launch opens at 1 ETH

Regardless of treasury size, regardless of which quote asset, regardless of
one pool or two — every launch's fully-diluted opening value is exactly 1
ETH. A creator depositing 10 ETH worth of treasury assets does not make
their launch open any higher. The treasury figure and the opening price are
two different numbers, on purpose.

## What you actually see

- **Opening price** — fixed, 1 ETH ÷ total supply, the same for every launch.
- **Backing per token** — the treasury's real, live value ÷ total supply.
  Can be anything, including zero. Never influences the opening price above.
- **Reachable with any token** vs. **holders only** — a quote asset either
  has a real, deep trading route from common assets, or it doesn't. Ballast
  marks the difference plainly rather than letting you find out mid-trade.

## What Ballast never does

No floor, no guarantee, no yield, no reward for depositing. A stock-quoted
launch's price is read live from the same public price feed the stock
token's own issuer uses — never smoothed, never projected forward, and
clearly marked when that feed is resting (a normal weekend/holiday pause)
versus genuinely stale.
